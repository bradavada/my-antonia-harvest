A note from the almanac

What farmers know

June: keep weeds down and conserve moisture. July: respond to heat, pests and labor shortages. August: prepare tools, stores and hands for the harvest. These are historically inspired principles, not verified quotations from an 1884 almanac.

A full field of wheat is not the same as a full granary. Someone must cut, bind, haul, and store it.
Literature matters

Whose work counts?

As you play, consider Ántonia's physical work, Ambrosch's decisions, Mrs. Shimerda's precarious position, and the help Jake and Otto provide the Burdens. Is the prairie an equal opportunity for every family?
